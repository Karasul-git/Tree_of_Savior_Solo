### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Double Slash: Critical**: Increases the critical chance of [Double Slash] by 7% per attribute level. (Swordsman 3rd Circle required)
- **Double Slash: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Double Slash] by 1% per attribute level. (Swordsman 3rd Circle required)
