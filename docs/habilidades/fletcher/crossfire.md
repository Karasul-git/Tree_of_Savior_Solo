### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Crossfire: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Crossfire] by 1% per attribute level. (Fletcher 1st Circle required)
- **Crossfire: Burn**: [Crossfire] gets a 10% chance per level of inflicting [Burn] on targets for 6 seconds. Burn damage is proportional to the character's physical attack. (Fletcher 1st Circle required)
