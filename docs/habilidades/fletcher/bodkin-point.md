### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Bodkin Point: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Bodkin Point] by 1% per attribute level. (Fletcher 1st Circle required)
- **Bodkin Point: Decreased Defense**: Further decreases an enemy's physical defense by 1% per attribute level when attacked by [Bodkin Point]. (Fletcher 1st Circle required)
