### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Headshot: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Headshot] by 1% per attribute level. (Musketeer 1st Circle required)
- **Headshot: Stun**: Enemies hit by [Headshot] have a 2.5% chance per attribute level to become afflicted with [Stun] for 3 seconds. (Musketeer 1st Circle required)
