### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Butt Stroke: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Butt Stroke] by 1% per attribute level. (Musketeer 1st Circle required)
- **Butt Stroke: Stun**: Enemies hit by [Butt Stroke] have a 1.5% chance per attribute level to become afflicted with [Stun] for 3 seconds. (Musketeer 1st Circle required)
