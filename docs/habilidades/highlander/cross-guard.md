### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Cross Guard: Extend Stagger**: Extends the duration of time when an enemy blocked by [Crossguard] remains staggered. Increases by 1 second per attribute level. (Highlander 1st Circle required)
- **Cross Guard: Enhance**: Increases the physical defense increase effect of [Cross Guard] by 1 per attribute level. (Lv3 Cross Guard required)
