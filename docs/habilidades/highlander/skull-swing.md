### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Skull Swing: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Skull Swing] by 1% per attribute level. (Highlander 3rd Circle required)
- **Skull Swing: No Jump**: Enemies cannot jump when attacked with [Skull Swing]. (Highlander 3rd Circle required)
- **Skull Swing: Remove Knockback**: [Skull Swing] does not push away enemies anymore. (Only applies if the attribute is set to ON) (Highlander 3rd Circle required)
