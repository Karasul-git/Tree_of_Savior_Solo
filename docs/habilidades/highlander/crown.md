### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Crown: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Crown] by 1% per attribute level. (Highlander 1st Circle required)
- **Crown: Reduce**: Increases the reduced amount of the enemy's INT and SPR from [Crown] by 2% per attribute level. (Highlander 1st Circle required)
