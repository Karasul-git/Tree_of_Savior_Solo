### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Synchro Thrusting: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Synchro Thrusting] by 1% per attribute level. (Hoplite 1st Circle required)
- **Synchro Thrusting: Pierce**: Decreases [Strike] damage by 10%, and increases [Pierce] damage by 10% per attribute level of [Synchro Thrusting]. (Hoplite 1st Circle required)
