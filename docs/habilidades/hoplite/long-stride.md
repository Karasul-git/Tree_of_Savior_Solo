### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Long Stride: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Long Stride] by 1% per attribute level. (Hoplite 2nd Circle required)
- **Long Stride: Decreased Cooldown**: Decreases the cooldown time of [Long Stride] by 10 seconds. (Hoplite 2nd Circle required)
