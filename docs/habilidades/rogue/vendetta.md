### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Vendetta: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Vendetta] by 1% per attribute level. (Rogue 1st Circle required)
- **Vendetta: Counter Attack**: Doubles the damage when hitting an enemy affected by the [Bleeding] status ailment with [Vendetta]. (Rogue 1st Circle required)
