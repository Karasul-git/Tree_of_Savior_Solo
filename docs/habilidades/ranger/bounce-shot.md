### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Bounce Shot: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Bounce Shot] by 1% per attribute level. (Ranger 3rd Circle required)
- **Bounce Shot: Slow**: Enemies hit by [Bounce Shot] have a 3% chance per attribute level to become afflicted with [Slow]. (Ranger 3rd Circle required)
