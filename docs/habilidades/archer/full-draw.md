### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Full Draw: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Full Draw] by 1% per attribute level. (Archer 1st Circle required)
- **Full Draw: Decreased Defense**: Decreases the physical defense of enemies by 10% per attribute level who are threaded with [Full Draw]. (Archer 1st Circle required)
- **Full Draw: Increased Knockback**: Increases the knockback power of [Full Draw] by 50. (Archer 1st Circle required)
