### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Smite: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Smite] by 1% per attribute level. (Paladin 1st Circle required)
- **Smite: Knockdown Damage**: Deals damage equal to 50% of physical attack to enemies knocked into the air by [Smite], on landing. (This effect only activates when the attribute is ON) (Paladin 1st Circle required)
