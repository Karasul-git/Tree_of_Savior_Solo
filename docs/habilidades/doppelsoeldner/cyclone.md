### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Cyclone: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Cyclone] by 1% per attribute level. (Doppelsoeldner 1st Circle required)
- **Cyclone: Resistance**: Increases the chance to block harmful effects by 15% per attribute level during [Cyclone]. (Lv. 3 Cyclone required)
- **Cyclone: Movement**: Allows movement when using [Cyclone]. (Doppelsoeldner 1st Circle required)
