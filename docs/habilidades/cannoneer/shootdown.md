### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Shootdown: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Shootdown] by 1% per attribute level. (Cannoneer 1st Circle required)
- **Shootdown: Falling**: Flying-type monsters hit by [Shootdown] will fall down on the ground for 4 seconds per attribute level. From Lv. 2, the duration decreases by 1.8 seconds per attribute level. (The Flying-type monsters who fall down can receive additional damage by Cannon Shot.) (Cannoneer 1st Circle required)
