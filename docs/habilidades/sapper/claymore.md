### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Claymore: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Claymore] by 1% per attribute level. (Sapper 1st Circle required)
- **Claymore: Splash**: Increases the AoE attack ratio of [Claymore] by 1 per attribute level. (Sapper 1st Circle required)
- **Claymore: Flying-type Compatibility**: Deals 10% additional damage per attribute level on Flying-type monsters with [Claymore]. (Sapper 1st Circle required)
