### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Punji Stake: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Punji Stake] by 1% per attribute level. (Sapper 1st Circle required)
- **Punji Stake: Knockdown Damage**: When an enemy launched by [Punji Stake] hits the ground, it and nearby enemies will be dealt 50% of physical attack as damage. (Sapper 1st Circle required)
