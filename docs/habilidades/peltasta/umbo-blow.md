### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Umbo Blow: Stun**: Enemies hit by [Umbo Blow] have a 5% chance per attribute level to become afflicted with [Stun] for 3 seconds. (Peltasta 1st Circle required)
- **Umbo Blow: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Umbo Blow] by 1% per attribute level. (Peltasta 1st Circle required)
- **[Umbo Blow]: Add Knockdown**: Enemies hit by [Umbo Blow] will fall down, increases the knockdown power by 40 per attribute level. (Peltasta 1st Circle required)
