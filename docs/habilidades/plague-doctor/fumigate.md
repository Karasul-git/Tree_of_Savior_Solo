### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Fumigate: Prevention**: Increases the chance that [Fumigate] blocks Lv3 or weaker status ailments for 10 seconds by 5% per attribute level. (Plague Doctor 1st Circle required)
- **Fumigate: Purification**: The range of [Fumigate] becomes a purification area for 10 seconds. Increases the ally's poison resistance by 10% per attribute level. (Plague Doctor 1st Circle required)
