### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Bloodletting: Hemostasis**: Increases the cycle time of the HP cost by 0.02 seconds per attribute level while [Bloodletting] is in effect. (Plague Doctor 1st Circle required)
- **Bloodletting: Substitute SP**: Each tick has a 10% chance per attribute level to reduce SP instead of HP while [Bloodletting] is in effect. (Plague Doctor 1st Circle required)
