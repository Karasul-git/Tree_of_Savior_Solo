### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Steed Charge: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Steed Charge] by 1% per attribute level. (Cataphract 1st Circle required)
- **Steed Charge: Critical**: Applies critical on [Steed Charge] on enemies afflicted with [Slow]. (Cataphract 1st Circle required)
- **Steed Charge: Remove Knockdown**: [Steed Charge] no longer knocks enemies down. (Only applies if the attribute is set to ON) (Cataphract 1st Circle required)
