### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Serpentine: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Serpentine] by 1% per attribute level. (Dragoon 1st Circle required)
- **Serpentine: Bleeding**: Enemies hit by [Serpentine] have a 2% chance per attribute level to become afflicted with [Bleeding] for 8 seconds. Bleeding damage is proportional to the character's physical attack. (Dragoon 1st Circle required)
