### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Gae Bulg: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Gae Bulg] by 1% per attribute level. (Dragoon 1st Circle required)
- **Gae Bulg: Defense Zone**: Creates a defense zone for 15 seconds where the spear is thrown. Increases physical defense and magic defense by 2% for party members within the zone. (Lv. 5 Gae Bulg required)
- **Gae Bulg: Attack Zone**: Creates an attack zone for 15 seconds where the spear is thrown. Increases physical attack and magic attack by 2% for party members within the zone. (Lv. 5 Gae Bulg required)
