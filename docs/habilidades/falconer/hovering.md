### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Hovering: Duration**: Increases the duration of [Hovering] by 3 seconds per attribute level. (Falconer 1st Circle required)
- **Hovering: Attack Speed Buff**: Your hawk will attack enemies faster while using [Hovering]. (Lv3 Hovering required)
- **Hovering: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Hovering] by 1% per attribute level. (Falconer 1st Circle required)
