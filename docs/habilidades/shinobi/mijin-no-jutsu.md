### Atributos da habilidade em 2016

- **Mijin no Jutsu: Enhance**: Increases the damage dealt on an enemy with [Mijin no Jutsu] by 1% per attribute level. (Shinobi 1st Circle required)
- **Mijin no Jutsu: Stealth**: Your character will hide for 5 seconds after using [Mijin no Jutsu]. Increases the duration from Lv2 by 1.5 seconds per attribute level. (Shinobi 1st Circle required)
