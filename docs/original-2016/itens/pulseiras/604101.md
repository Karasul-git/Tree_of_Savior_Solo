## Descrição

> - Physical Attack 21
> - Critical Rate 37
> - Accuracy 20
> - Avoiding an enemy's attack will trigger (Frenzy) for 5 seconds
> - Increases physical attack by 235, movement speed by 10 and reduces physical defense by 88 for 30 seconds when this effect is stacked 8 times.
> According to a legend, a warrior obtained this bracelet at the cost of relinquishing his sanity and humanity for pure frenzy, in order to seal away a very strong Demon King.
