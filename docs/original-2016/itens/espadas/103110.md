## Descrição

> - 5% chance to inflict (Bleeding) for 30 seconds on enemies when using a skill attack
> - A basic attack will hit 5 successive times every 5 times you apply (Bleeding) to enemies
> - 50% reduced damage of (Ghost) armor-type targets
> This sword whose shape resembles the jaws of a shark can do much more than just cutting.
