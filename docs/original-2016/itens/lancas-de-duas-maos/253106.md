## Descrição

> - STR 20
> - Attack Range 10
> - Max Stamina 10
> - 33% chance to lower an enemy's Stamina by 1 and inflict (Cripple) for 30 seconds
> - Stacking (Cripple) 10 times will lower a target's movement speed by 20 for 10 seconds
> Anyone hit by this spear will suddenly be limping.
