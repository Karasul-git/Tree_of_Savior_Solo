## Descrição

> - AoE Attack Ratio 1
> - Fire Property Resistance 40
> - Increases all Cryomancer skill levels by 2
> - 15% chance to inflict (Freeze) on an enemy for 3 seconds with an Ice property skill
> Some say that the Cryomancer Master jokingly challenged the Pyromancer Master to fight him with this staff using ice magic while he was barehanded.
