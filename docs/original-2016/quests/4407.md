## Início

> Heed the words of the Old Owl Sculpture and bring the Memory Fragment to the Worrying Owl Sculpture at Bandyma Crossroad.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Worrying Owl Sculpture.

## Durante

> In order to defeat Gaigalas, you need the power of the Owl Sculptures that fell asleep without completing their magic. Awaken the memories of the Sleeping Owl Sculptures to complete the magic that will weaken Gaigalas.

**Objetivo:** Awaken the Memories of the Sleeping Owl Sculptures

## Fim

> Completed the magic that will weaken Gaigalas. Return and tell it to the Worrying Owl Sculpture.

**Objetivo:** Report to the Worrying Owl Sculpture
