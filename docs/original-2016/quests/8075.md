# Healer Lady's Worry (1)

[Quests de 2016](README.md)

| Campo | Valor |
| --- | --- |
| Nível | 10 |
| Nível necessário | 1 |
| NPC inicial | Healer Lady |
| Mapa | Miners' Village |

## Início

> You need to go to the Crystal Mines as told by the goddess in the bishop's dream but you can't just leave the refugees. Help the Healer Lady to treat the injured people.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Healer Lady

## Durante

> Healer Lady is asking a favor for you to bring the other refugees. Bring the couple near the Statue of Goddess Zemyna to the Healer Lady.

**Objetivo:** Bring the refugees

## Fim

> Brought back the refugees safely. Talk to the Healer Lady.

**Objetivo:** Turn over the refugees to the Healer Lady

## Recompensas

Recebe todos:

- [Lv1 EXP Card](../itens/consumiveis/640080.md) × 1
