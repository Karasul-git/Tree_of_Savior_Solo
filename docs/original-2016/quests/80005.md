## Início

**Objetivo:** Talk to Grave Keeper Sigis

## Durante

> You are ready to look at the sacred object of the goddess which was created by the Believers of the Order of the Tree of Truth. Bring the spell doll and check the sacred object of the goddess at Sielva Pray Room.

**Objetivo:** Investigate the goddess' sacred object which was created by the Order of the Tree of Truth

## Fim

> You've destroyed the barrier, but you got near the sacred object, the spell doll was destroyed. Return to Sigis and tell him about it.

**Objetivo:** Report to Grave Keeper Sigis
