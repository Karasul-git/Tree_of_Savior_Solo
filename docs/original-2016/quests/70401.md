## Início

> Mage Melchioras says he has returned to this place to gather the Revelators and stop another Kruvina from being created in Delmore Castle. Keep listening to Mage Melchioras.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Mage Melchioras

## Durante

> You have decided to help Melchioras. Visit the spots marked on the map and use the compass to find the objects under the protection cast. The compass is activated only near the cast.

**Objetivo:** Use the compass to search for hidden artifacts

## Fim

> You have found the objects under the protection cast. Relay their location to Mage Melchioras.

**Objetivo:** Report to Mage Melchioras
