## Durante

> Follower Tomas told you that it is necessary to defeat the Unknocker that is guarding the altar in order to disable the barrier. Attack the Unknocker when Follower Tomas lures it.

**Objetivo:** Defeat Unknocker

## Fim

> You defeated the Unknocker that was guarding the altar. Talk to Follower Tomas again.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Tomas
