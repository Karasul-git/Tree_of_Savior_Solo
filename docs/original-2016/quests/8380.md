## Início

> Various writings on how to prepare when the demons attack are engraved on the epitaph. Read the epitaph.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Guardian's Stone Statue

## Durante

> The Guardian's Stone Statue told you that you should defeat sleeping Guardians using condensed magic before they wake up. Collect the condensed magic.

**Objetivo:** Collect the condensed magic source

## Fim

> You collected enough. Return to the Guardian's Stone Statue.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Guardian's Stone Statue
