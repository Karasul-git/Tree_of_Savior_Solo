**Objetivo:** Talk to the Guide Owl.

## Durante

> The Guide Owl Sculpture tells you that the branch can purify the monsters and their energies. You should absorb the purified energy and collect it.

**Objetivo:** Absorb the purified energy

## Fim

> Letas' Sacred Branch purified and absorbed enough energy. Return to the Guide Owl Sculpture.

**Objetivo:** Report to the Guide Owl Sculpture
