## Início

> The Pilgrim Agatas is waiting for someone's help at Sutatis Trade Route.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Pilgrim Agatas

## Durante

> The one who introduced himself as Demon Lord Hauberk is doing his best to save the Pilgrim Agatas. Collect black herbs to release the paralysis poison from Agatas.

**Objetivo:** Collect Black Herbs

## Fim

> As Demon Lord Hauberk requested, you've collected enough black herbs. Return to Hauberk.

**Objetivo:** Hand it over to Demon Lord Hauberk

## Recompensas

Recebe todos:

- [Lv5 EXP Card](../itens/consumiveis/640085.md) × 2
