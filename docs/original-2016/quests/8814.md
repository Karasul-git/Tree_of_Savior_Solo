## Início

> Guard Gofden is waiting for someone's help at Roxona Market, the Petrified City.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Guard Gofden

## Durante

> Guard Gofden asked you to destroy the records of the rebels that were left neglected for a long time. You can find the records of the rebels from the commerce district.

**Objetivo:** Destroy the records of the rebels

## Fim

> As Guard Gofden requested of you, you've destroyed most of the rebel's records. Let's go back to Gofden.

**Objetivo:** Report to Guard Gofden
