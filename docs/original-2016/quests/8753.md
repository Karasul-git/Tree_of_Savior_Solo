> Hunter Talus asked you to make Fire Hardened Arrowheads. Defeat the Infroburks at the Shiluma Altar Site, and use the leftover flame to heat the arrow that Tallus gave you.

**Objetivo:** Make Fire Hardened arrowheads

## Fim

> Made the heated arrowheads. Give them to Talus.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Hunter Talus
