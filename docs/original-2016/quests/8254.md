## Início

> Various writings on how to prepare when the demons attack are engraved on the epitaph. Read the epitaph.

**Objetivo:** Check the epitaph of the Royal Mausoleum

## Fim

> When a Royal Mausoleum Guardian forgets its duty, its power source gets put into a container and returned to the Royal Mausoleum. Defeat the Guardians and obtain their power sources, then put them into the containers.

**Objetivo:** Get the power source from the Guardian and dedicate it to the chest
