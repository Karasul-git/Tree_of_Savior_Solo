## Tarefa

- Defeat the Glutton that was once a Tree Root Crystal (0 / 1)

## Início

> There is a gigantic Spoiled Tree Root Crystal at the space towards Altar Way. It looks different from the other Tree Root Crystals. Get closer to it and look closely.

**Objetivo:** Investigate the Tree Root Crystals on the way to Altar Way.

## Durante

> The Tree Root Crystal suddenly transformed to a Glutton! Defeat the Glutton!

**Objetivo:** Defeat the Shape-shifting Glutton
