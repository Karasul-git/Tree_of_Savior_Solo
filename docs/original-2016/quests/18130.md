## Início

> Found the villager, but he seems hurt. Ask what happened.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Injured Villager

## Durante

> The villager says he was attacked while on his way to check on the portal. Go and check if the portal at Nugria Sanctum is open for the villager.

**Objetivo:** Check the altar at Nugria Sanctum

## Fim

> Purified the Holy Pond, but the portal at Nugria Sanctum did not open. Return to the Injured Villager and ask what to do.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Injured Villager
