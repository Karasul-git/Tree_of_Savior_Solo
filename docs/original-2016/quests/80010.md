## Início

> It seems that Gintas has one more thing to request to you. Talk to Gintas.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Believer Gintas

## Durante

> Gintas says the once-believers of the Order have resurrected, possibly by the power of Margiris. Defeat the resurrected believers in the Seima Stone Chamber and then use the flint to burn the bodies.

**Objetivo:** Cremate the Believer who has resurrected

## Fim

> As you were burning the resurrected Believer, the spirit of the Believer appeared. It seems that the spirit has something to say to you.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the spirit of the Believer that appeared
