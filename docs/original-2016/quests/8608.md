## Início

> Follower Alfonsas of Tenet Garden is waiting for someone's help.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Alfonsas

## Durante

> Follower Alfonsas said that scribbling on the Demon Summoning Circles alters them greatly. Avoid the monsters and scribble the circles.

**Objetivo:** Write over the Demon Summoning Circles

## Fim

> A little vandalism on the summoning circle absorbed back the summoned demons. It was small but a good try. Return and let Follower Alfonsas know about it.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Alfonsas
