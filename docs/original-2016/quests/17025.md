## Início

> If you had saved all of the sealed souls in the sealed stones past the Mage Tower, you will be able to receive the last mission from the sealed stone on the way to the Great Hall of Keturidu.

**Objetivo:** Find the Sealed Stone

## Durante

> If you do the seal stone's favors, then you will be able to find out the identity of those trapped inside the seal stones. Kill the monsters guarding around the seal stone as requested.

**Objetivo:** Defeat the monsters nearby

## Fim

> Defeated all the monsters around. Return to the seal stone.

**Objetivo:** Report to the Sealed Stone
