## Início

> Liaison Officer Niels say Jonas' family is looking for you. Talk to Ahylas in the Excavation Camp.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Ahylas Jonas

## Durante

> Ahylas needs to prepare the legacy of Great King Zachariel for the chosen one. He needs the Shiny Tree Sap which is called the tears of Goddess Zemyna. Go to Zeme Highland to obtain the sap.

**Objetivo:** Collect the Shiny Tree Sap.

## Fim

> Got enough tree sap. Bring it to Ahylas.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Ahylas
