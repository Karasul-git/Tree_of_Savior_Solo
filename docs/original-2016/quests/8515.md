## Início

> Follower Donatas is waiting for the Revelator at the 1st floor of the church to hand over the scroll that turns oneself into a demon.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Donatas

## Durante

> Follower Donatas told you to gain some confidence by defeating Pawndel and Pawnd, so that you can transform into a demon using the demon transform scroll. After transforming yourself into a demon, talk to Pawndel and Pawnd, lure them to Apsauga Altar and defeat them.

**Objetivo:** Lure Pawndel and Pawnd to the Apsauga Altar

## Fim

> You defeated Pawndel and Pawnd after luring them to the altar. Talk to Follower Donatas.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Donatas
