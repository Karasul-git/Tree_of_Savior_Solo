## Início

> Mirta seems to be severely injured. Ask him why he is injured and where the materials to be sent to Kepeck are.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Mercenary Mirta

## Durante

> Mercenary Mirta says that he lost the materials from monsters' attacks. Retrieve the materials and send them to Kepeck on behalf of Mirta.

**Objetivo:** Retrieve the materials to be sent to Kepeck.

## Fim

> It seems that you retrieved most of the materials. Give them to the Historian Kepeck at Apvija Hill.

**Objetivo:** Find Historian Kepeck at Apvija Hill
