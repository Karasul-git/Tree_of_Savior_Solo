## Durante

> The Kabbalist Master told you that in order to become a Kabbalist, you should be able to undertand all numbers that are hidden in all properties in the world. Use the crystal sphere that has appeared at Cranto Coast and defeat the monster which the number on the sphere indicates.

**Objetivo:** Defeat the monster indicated by the number displayed on the crystal sphere

**Objetivo:** Report to the Kabbalist Master
