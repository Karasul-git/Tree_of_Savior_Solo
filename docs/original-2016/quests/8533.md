> Follower Algis is waiting for the Revelator in the Tenet Church 2F.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Algis

## Durante

> Follower Algis says you need to defeat any pursuing demons. Use the Auka Altar to defeat demons and keep them from chasing you.

**Objetivo:** Charge the low level spirit crystal

## Fim

> Activated the altar and shifted the demons' interest to the altar. Return to Follower Algis.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Algis
