## Início

> Fortunately, it seems that Sarma didn't notice that the ingredients have changed. Listen from her what she is trying to do next.

**Objetivo:** Talk with Sarma

## Durante

> Sarma told you that since she prepared everything, you should just have to activate the ductile magic field. Put the ingredients of the mixed alchemy above and in front of the tombstone that locks the magical power, release the locked magical power to make the ingredients ductile.

**Objetivo:** Use the ductile magic field to create ductile ingredients

## Fim

> You've completed the liquid which Sarma told you about by using the ductile magic circle. Although, you've mixed some fake ingredients, it looks normal from the appearances. Go back to Sarma and hand over the liquid to her.

**Objetivo:** Hand over the completed liquid to Sarma
