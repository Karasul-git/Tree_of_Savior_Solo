## Início

> Tiberius is waiting for someone's help in the Tenet Church 1F.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Tiberius

## Durante

> Tiberius told you to obtain Vicious Essences by defeating demons, and then purify them into Purified Essences at the Altar of Purification, so he can complete his Holy Bomb.

**Objetivo:** Obtained the Purified Essences.

## Fim

> You have collected enough Purified Essences as Tiberius requested of you. Return to Tiberius.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Tiberius
