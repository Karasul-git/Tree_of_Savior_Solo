## Início

> Watcher Kenneth is waiting for someone's help at Nefritas Cliff.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Watcher Kenneth

## Durante

> Watcher Kenneth told you to weaken the demons to less than half HP, then use the holy powers of the barrier to sever their souls and defeat them.

**Objetivo:** Defeat severed demons' souls

## Fim

> Defeated enough severed souls in Pumpura Hill. Report to Follower Kayetonas.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Kayetonas
