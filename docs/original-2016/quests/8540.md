[Quests de 2016](README.md)

| --- | --- |

| Mapa | Nefritas Cliff |

## Início

> Watcher Kenneth is waiting for someone's help at Nefritas Cliff.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Watcher Kenneth

## Durante

> Watcher Kenneth is worried about the Demon Summoning Circles in Mairunas Knoll. Defeat the demons nearby Mairunas Knoll and remove the summoning circles.

**Objetivo:** Remove the Demon Summoning Circles

## Fim

> Removed the Demon Summoning Circles as requested by Watcher Kenneth. Tell Follower Kayetonas at Flower Greeting Hill that there were Demon Summoning Circles.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Kayetonas
