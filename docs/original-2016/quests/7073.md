## Tarefa

- Desert Chupacabra (0 / 7)

## Início

> Mercenary Toby is looking for someone who can help him. Talk to Toby

**Objetivo:** Talk to Mercenary Toby

## Durante

> Toby is looking for someone who can help him with simple jobs. But, since he can't give the jobs to anyone, he needs to test your skills. Show him that you can defeat Desert Chupabra and Zinute.

**Objetivo:** Prove your skills

## Fim

> Toby seems to be very satisfied. Return to Toby.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Mercenary Toby
