## Início

> Merchant Davio told you that he needs one more herb to complete the medicine, but he already sent off the Medicine Dealer to get the herb. Meet the Medicine Dealer at Kapas Highway.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Medicine Dealer

## Durante

> The Medicine Dealer told you that all other ingredients is ready, except for Canyon Metos. Collect Canyon Metoses in Metos Field in order to complete the medicine that will bring back Jonas' conscious.

**Objetivo:** Collect Canyon Metos

## Fim

> You have enough Canyon Metos. Return to the Medicine Dealer and complete the medicine that will bring back Jonas' conscious.

**Objetivo:** Talk to the Medicine Dealer
