## Início

> Treasure Hunter of Stele Road is waiting for someone's help.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Treasure Hunter Eden

## Durante

> Treasure Hunter Eden asked you to collect the sap of Stumpy Tree for its worth. Attach sap collectors to Stumpy Trees and defeat them afterwards to get their sap.

**Objetivo:** Collect Stumpy Tree Sap

## Fim

> Collected enough Stumpy Tree Sap. Return to Treasure Hunter Eden.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Treasure Hunter Eden
