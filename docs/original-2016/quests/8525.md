## Início

> Vaidas is waiting for help at the Tenet Church B1.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Vaidas

## Durante

> Vaidas told you to charge the empty Holy Stone with the lives of the demons. He told you that when you place the stone and lure demons near it, the stone will automatically absorb the lives of the demons.

**Objetivo:** Charge the Holy Stone

## Fim

> You successfully charged the Holy Stone as Vaidas requested of you. Return to Vaidas.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Vaidas
