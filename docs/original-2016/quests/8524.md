## Durante

> Tiberius wants to test the Holy Bombs on Glizardon. Drink the Namott Holy Water to hide yourself, and then put the Holy Bomb onto a Glizardon's back.

**Objetivo:** Defeat Glizardon using the Holy Bomb

> Glizardon died right away after the Holy Bomb on it's back exploded. Return to Tiberius and tell him about it.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Follower Tiberius
