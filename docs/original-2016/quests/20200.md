## Início

> Epigraphist Raymond of Stele Road is waiting for someone's help.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Epigraphist Raymond

## Durante

> Raymond asked you to collect pieces of the Dusty Fragments from Gaisra Hall Site. He told you to defeat the Tree Ambuloes and get the Dusty Fragments back if they happen to steal any from you.

**Objetivo:** Collect the Dusty Fragments

## Fim

> Gathered enough Dusty Fragments. Give them to Raymond.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Epigraphist Raymond
