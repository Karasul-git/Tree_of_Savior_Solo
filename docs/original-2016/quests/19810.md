## Início

**Objetivo:** Receive the Sanctum's Blessing

## Durante

> You will need Pure Water in order to receive the blessing from the sanctum. Get some Pure Water from the stepping stones in Ryskus City.

**Objetivo:** Obtain some Pure Water from Ryskus City

## Fim

> I've brought Pure Water. Go to the sanctum and pray.

**Objetivo:** Receive the Sanctum's Blessing
