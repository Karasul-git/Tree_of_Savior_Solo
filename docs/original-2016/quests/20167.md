## Início

> Seems like Ahylas is matching a crystal to release the seal so that you can approach Great King Zachariel's legacy.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Ahylas Jonas

## Durante

> Ahylas says he needs Amalas to make the crystal for unsealing. Get Amalas from Pamirstas Highland. Amalas is difficult to collect so be careful.

**Objetivo:** Collect Canyon Amalas

## Fim

> Got enough Amalas. Give it to Ahylas.

**Objetivo:** Give it to Ahylas
