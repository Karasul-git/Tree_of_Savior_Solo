## Início

> Talk to the priest

**Objetivo:** Talk to Priest Prosit

## Durante

> There are many orbs that are spreading the curse at various places in the Sanctuary. You can't destroy them normally so collect them and bring them to Priest Prosit.

**Objetivo:** Collect the curse-spreading orbs

## Fim

> You've collected all the cursed orbs. Hand them over to Priest Prosit.

**Objetivo:** Hand them over to Priest Prosit

## Recompensas

Recebe todos:

- [Priest Prosit's Research](../itens/itens-de-quest/663007.md) × 1
- [Lv7 EXP Card](../itens/consumiveis/640113.md) × 1
