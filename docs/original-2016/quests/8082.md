## Início

> You need to remove the wagons in order to enter the mines. Talk to Vaidotas about how to remove them.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Vaidotas

## Durante

> Vaidotas says the wagons can be destroyed with explosives. But the Vubbes could attack when they hear the explosives, so you must be cautious.

**Objetivo:** Defeat any Vubbe that assembles after hearing the explosives

## Fim

> You removed the wagons and can finally enter the Crystal Mines. Meet Vaidotas at Crystal Mine 1F.

**Objetivo:** Talk to Vaidotas in Crystal Mine 1F
